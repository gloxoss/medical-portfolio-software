# Phase 8 — Executive Summary

## MediCore HIS — Non-Technical Overview

---

## The Problem

Doctors in private clinics — especially solo practitioners and small polyclinic networks — are drowning in paperwork. For every hour spent with a patient, physicians spend two hours documenting the visit in their records. This "pajama time" (working on notes after the clinic closes) contributes to burnout, reduces the number of patients a doctor can see, and leads to billing errors that leak 5–15% of clinic revenue.

The alternatives available today are either **too expensive** (enterprise systems like Athenahealth at $350–800/month per doctor, requiring 6–12 months to implement) or **too risky** (cheap scripts bought online for $39–199 one-time, but built on outdated technology with no security, no support, and no AI capability).

There is nothing in between: an affordable, modern, intelligent clinic management system.

---

## The Solution

**MediCore** is a cloud-based Hospital Information System that handles the full clinic workflow — from patient registration and appointment scheduling through clinical encounter documentation and billing — with an AI copilot built into the core of the product.

### How It Works (Plain English)

1. **Patient arrives** → Receptionist registers them in 90 seconds (name, phone, date of birth)
2. **Appointment booked** → The patient gets an SMS confirmation
3. **Patient checks in** → A queue token appears on the waiting room TV screen
4. **Doctor sees patient** → Opens the encounter form, records vital signs, types brief notes
5. **AI copilot helps** → The doctor types shorthand notes; the AI expands them into professional, structured medical documentation (saving 30–45 minutes per day)
6. **Encounter signed off** → An invoice is automatically created; the receptionist collects payment
7. **End of day** → The clinic owner sees a dashboard showing revenue by location, appointments, and AI usage

### What Makes MediCore Different

- **AI-native**: The AI copilot is built into the product from day one — not a bolt-on feature that costs extra
- **Affordable**: Starting at $39–49/month for a solo doctor — 90% cheaper than enterprise alternatives
- **Multi-location**: One system manages all clinics in a network — no separate installations per location
- **Zero setup burden**: Self-service onboarding in 30 minutes. No IT team required

## The Market

The ambulatory (outpatient) EHR market is worth **$5.7–6.8 billion globally** and growing at 5.4% annually. Clinics are actively moving to cloud systems, with 81% of new EHR deployments being cloud-based.

MediCore initially targets **Morocco and francophone Africa** — a region with thousands of private clinics but almost zero modern, AI-powered cloud options available at an affordable price point.
